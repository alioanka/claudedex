How DEX Trading Works (click to expand)

  • What it does: Spot trades on EVM DEXes (Uniswap V2/V3, SushiSwap, PancakeSwap) across Ethereum, BSC, Polygon, Arbitrum, and Base.
  • Routing: The engine quotes across every router in supported_dexs and picks the best fill net of fees and slippage.
  • MEV protection: When enabled, transactions submit through the Flashbots private relay so frontrunners and sandwichers cannot see your order in the public mempool.
  • Gas ceiling: max_gas_price is a hard upper bound in gwei. If the network base fee exceeds this, the bot waits rather than overpay.
  • Slippage: max_slippage_bps is the tolerance applied to the AMM quote at execution. 50 bps = 0.5%.
  • DRY_RUN: When DRY_RUN is ON, every executor in this module simulates trades instead of broadcasting. The banner above always reflects the live source of truth from /api/modules/dex/dry-run.
  • Kill switches: logs/.killswitch (global) and logs/.pause_dex (per-module) force all writes through the simulator path; the emergency-stop button writes the global file.
